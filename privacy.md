# UC Live — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-18_

UC Live ("the extension") is built to keep everything on your own machine.

**What we collect:** Nothing. UC Live does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal
data, browsing history, page content, or credentials.

**Where your data lives:**
- Page captures are stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB. They never leave your device
  unless you explicitly export them to your own Downloads folder.
- The Live Bridge feature reads and writes only a single folder that you explicitly select on your
  own computer via Chrome's File System Access API. Page snapshots and commands pass through that
  local folder. Nothing is sent to any server.

**Network activity:** The extension makes no network requests to transmit your data. The only
outbound request is an optional license check to ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) to verify
subscription status for the paid Live Bridge feature; this check does not include any page content
or personal data.

**Credentials:** UC Live never reads, stores, or transmits your passwords. Password fields are
redacted in captures, and the extension never types passwords on your behalf.

**Your control:** You choose which folder the bridge uses, you can stop the bridge at any time, and
uninstalling the extension deletes all locally stored captures.
